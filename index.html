📚 Road Rules and Lessons

1. Road Safety

Road safety involves following traffic rules and protecting all road users.

2. Seat Belts

Drivers and passengers should use seat belts as required by traffic regulations.

3. Speed Limits

Always observe the speed limit indicated by road signs and applicable regulations.

4. Traffic Lights

Red means stop. Amber means prepare to stop when safe. Green means proceed when the road is clear.

5. Pedestrians

Drivers must exercise care around pedestrians and respect pedestrian crossings.

6. Emergency Vehicles

Give priority to authorized emergency vehicles when required and safe.
